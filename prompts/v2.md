You are helping me plan a UK public sector AI Guidance Explorer.
## Product intent
Build a tool that:
1. Brings together available sources of AI guidance used in the UK public sector into one browsable catalogue.
2. Lets people explore guidance by organisation, category, theme, audience, and related facets.
3. Connects common AI adoption / governance questions to relevant guidance, with direct citations and links.
4. Starts simple: in an early version, users can browse a curated FAQ and see answers grounded in the catalogue; later, support asking free-text questions with retrieved citations.
This is a planning exercise, not an implementation yet. Optimise for a credible MVP that a small GDS/public-sector team could ship and maintain.
## Constraints & context
- Audience: people working on AI adoption in UK government / public sector (policy, delivery, assurance, data, security, legal/privacy).
- Tone & UX: clear, trustworthy, GOV.UK-like; authority and provenance matter more than clever chat UX.
- Content must be attributable: every FAQ answer should cite specific guidance items with links (and ideally section/page where possible).
- Prefer curated quality over exhaustive coverage in v1.
- Assume guidance sources are mostly public web pages/PDFs from official bodies (CDDO/DSIT, GDS, ICO, NCSC, departmental guidance, etc.). Confirm what should be in/out of scope.
- Be explicit about risks: stale guidance, conflicting advice, overconfident answers, maintenance burden.
## What I need from you
Produce a concise product plan with these sections:
### 1. Problem statement
Who has what problem today, and why a catalogue + FAQ-with-citations is the right response.
### 2. Users & primary use cases
3–5 personas / jobs-to-be-done, ranked for MVP.
### 3. MVP definition
- In scope / out of scope
- Core user journeys:
  - Browse catalogue by facets
  - Browse FAQ → answer + citations
  - (Optional stretch) Search / ask a question → retrieved answers with citations
- Non-goals for v1
### 4. Information architecture
- Proposed metadata schema for a guidance item
- Facets/filters (organisation, theme, category, etc.)
- How FAQ entries relate to guidance items
- How to handle duplicates, superseded docs, and conflicting guidance
### 5. Content strategy
- Initial source list (named orgs/collections to include first)
- Curation vs automation approach for v1
- Update/freshness process
- Suggested starter FAQ themes (10–20 example questions)
### 6. Trust & answer quality
- Citation requirements
- How answers should be written (summary + links, not replacing official policy)
- Disclaimers and escalation paths (“talk to your DPO / SIRO / etc.”)
### 7. Technical approach (lightweight)
- Suggested architecture options for:
  - catalogue data store
  - browse/filter UI
  - FAQ linking
  - later retrieval-augmented Q&A
- Pros/cons for a small team; recommend one path for MVP
### 8. Delivery plan
Phased roadmap (v0 content spike → v1 browse+FAQ → v2 search/Q&A), rough effort assumptions, and open decisions I still need to make.
### 9. Decision checklist
End with a short list of clarifying questions I should answer before committing to build.
## Working style
- Be concrete and opinionated; recommend defaults.
- Prefer public-sector delivery norms: small increments, maintainability, accessibility, clear ownership.
- Do not invent a large platform. Prefer the simplest system that makes guidance discoverable and FAQ answers citable.
- Call out unknowns instead of papering over them.
