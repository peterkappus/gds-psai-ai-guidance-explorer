# Planning questions

## Users & jobs

- Who is the primary user in v1: GDS/CDDO staff, departmental AI leads, delivery teams, policy, legal/privacy, or all of them?
- What’s the job-to-be-done: “find the right policy”, “answer an adoption FAQ with citations”, “compare guidance across orgs”, or “show gaps”?
- What does “done” look like for a user session (e.g. leave with 2–3 linked sources they’re confident are authoritative)?

## Scope of “guidance”

- What’s in scope: CDDO/DSIT AI frameworks, GDS service standards, ICO, NCSC, Cabinet Office, DHSC/NHS, MoJ, local government, arm’s-length bodies?
- Document types: strategies, playbooks, standards, risk frameworks, case studies, blog posts, parliamentary reports?
- Only official published guidance, or also draft / consultation / archived?
- UK-wide only, or also devolved administrations (Scotland/Wales/NI)?

## Browse & taxonomy

- What facets matter most: organisation, theme (risk, ethics, procurement, data, security, assurance), audience, maturity stage, document type, last updated?
- Do you need a curated taxonomy, or auto-tagging from content?
- How do you handle overlapping labels and conflicting advice across orgs?

## FAQ → guidance

- Is the FAQ curated by humans, generated from guidance, or hybrid?
- Early version: static Q&A with citations only, or also free-text search / Q&A over the corpus?
- How strict should citations be (exact page/section vs whole document)?
- How do you handle “no good answer” / conflicting sources?

## Trust & governance of the product itself

- Who owns accuracy and updates?
- How do you signal authority/freshness (publisher, date, status, supersedes)?
- Any accessibility / GOV.UK design system expectations?
- Can content leave the public web (embeddings, third-party LLM APIs), or must it stay on-prem / Crown-hosting friendly?

## Data & maintenance

- Manual catalogue vs scrape/RSS vs API vs mix?
- Update cadence and change detection?
- Minimum metadata schema for each item?
- Licensing / linking constraints?

## MVP & success

- What’s explicitly out of scope for v1?
- Success metrics: time-to-answer, citation click-through, curator effort, coverage of top N FAQs?
- Who will curate the first corpus and FAQ set?
